How it works

  1. Your webcam

    MediaPipe finds 21 points on each hand, on your Mac, every frame.

  2. Points become a gesture

    Finger angles decide the pose: open palm, fist, point or two fingers. Movement over time makes swipes, holds and pinches.

  3. The app in front decides

    Zephyr checks which app is in front and runs that app's action, or the one you recorded.

Camera video never leaves your Mac. Air writing sends only the strokes you draw, so they can be read as text.

Install

  1. Download Zephyr.dmg and open it.Drag Zephyr into Applications, then open it from there.
  2. Allow it the first time.Zephyr isn't notarized yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway next to Zephyr and enter your password.
  3. Grant the camera and Accessibility.Allow the camera, then turn on Zephyr under Accessibility so it can move the pointer and press keys.
  4. Raise both palms.Zephyr lives in the menu bar. Arm it with both palms and start pointing.
